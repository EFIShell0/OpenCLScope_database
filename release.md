## VulkanScope parity — Stage 8: Network & report evidence

- Internet settings automatically refresh request-scoped Cloudflare observations at most once per minute while the panel is visible. Offline/failed/stale observations are explicitly labeled; unobserved addresses and DNS are never fabricated.
- IP Show/Hide remains user-initiated and automatically hides the address after 15 seconds or when the panel changes/closes. Nothing is stored in reports or D1.
- OpenCL report platform/device/extension/profile details gain keyboard-accessible, cancellable animated accordions and reduced-motion behavior.
- `rules.md`, static asset release marker, 3.0.8 Android producer gate, verified archive snapshots and immutable OpenCL registry/Vulkan layout references are preserved.
- This stage does not claim full visual or operational parity with VulkanScope. Refer to the included comparison and QA files.
