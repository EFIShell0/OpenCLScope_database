About

OpenCLScope is not affiliated with the Khronos Group and is not an official Khronos Group project.

OpenCLScope is an OpenCL™ capability and device inspection utility for Android. It reports information exposed by the active OpenCL™ implementation, preserving unavailable, unknown and error states without inferring hardware support.
